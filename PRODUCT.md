# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (download site in `docs/`); the app itself is a native macOS menu-bar app (SwiftUI, `Sources/Keytone/`).

## Users
Any Mac user who types and wants it to feel nicer: the sound of a mechanical keyboard and mouse while typing on a laptop or a quiet keyboard. Comes for pleasure and comfort, not specs. Russian- and English-speaking.

## Product Purpose
Keytone plays a real mechanical-keyboard sound on every keypress and a click on mouse buttons and vertical scroll. It lives only in the menu bar. Success: someone installs it, forgets it's an app, and simply enjoys typing more.

## Positioning
It sounds natural: real switch recordings, a separate press and release sound per key, slight pitch and stereo variation, fast typing stays distinct instead of turning into a wash, and it barely uses the Mac (≈1% CPU idle). Free and open source.

## Operating Context
- Downloaded from GitHub Releases as `Keytone.zip`, moved to Applications.
- Not signed by Apple: first launch requires System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
- Requires the Input Monitoring permission to hear keypresses.
- All settings live in a small menu-bar panel; there is no main window.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Five bundled sound packs: Creamy Legendary, Zoom TKL Morandi, Keychron Q3 Blue, Wooting 60HE, Rainy 75 Pro.
- Three mouse sounds (Silent, Gaming, Retro); scroll sound for vertical scrolling only, can be muted separately.
- Import of Mechvibes packs (v1 and v2, folder or .zip).
- Options: release sound, ignore auto-repeat, stereo panning, pitch variation, launch at login.
- macOS 14+, Apple Silicon and Intel, ~3 MB.
- Records nothing, sends nothing.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Keytone. Icon: keycap with a waveform legend (`Assets/logo.svg`).
- Site is bilingual RU/EN with a language switch; the browser language picks the default.
- Voice should be warm and human: plain first-person-ish language, no marketing clichés.

## Evidence on Hand
- The app, its source and releases at github.com/aristocratonly/keytone.
- No testimonials, user counts, press or reviews exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Sound first: everything serves how natural typing feels and sounds.
2. Invisible when not wanted: no windows, minimal settings, near-zero load.
3. Honest: no fabricated claims; say plainly what the app does and needs (permissions, unsigned build).
4. Open and free.
