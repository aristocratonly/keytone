## Scope
Download site for Keytone (`docs/index.html`, RU/EN), mode: Persuade. The menu-bar panel (`Sources/Keytone/MenuUI.swift`, Operate) carries the same world quietly.

## Audience and action
Any Mac user. Believe: typing on a Mac can sound like a real mechanical keyboard, naturally, for free. Act: download Keytone.zip. Proof: the visitor types on the page and hears it.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Keytone as a stack of colored index cards typed on a two-color ribbon typewriter; every keypress leaves a mark. Refuses the dark SaaS hero with glowing product shot.

OWN-WORLD: Sky-blue card stock ground with faint blue rules and one red header rule; cards in goldenrod, mint and rose stock; black and red ribbon ink with uneven inking; typed headings (PT Mono with ink jitter), Golos Text for reading; index tabs as navigation and pack selector; a red rubber stamp as the primary action; punched holes and a paper clip as the only ornament.

STORY: The visitor sees a card being typed, types themselves and hears the pack, switches packs on the card's tabs, stamps Download, reads short single-thought cards on features, install and questions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One large blue index card nearly full width. Top edge: index tabs for the three demo packs. Typed headline across the ruled lines, a live typing line with caret beneath it, the red stamp "Скачать для macOS" lower right on the card, requirements typed small along the bottom rule.

FORM: index cards and typewriter ribbon, list position 5. seed:c5af35ba4a8abdf0. Raises: one card one thought (from airport wayfinding); install as a strict numbered margin column 01-04 (from origami sequence).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Cited adaptations
- Hero card stock: near-white index stock (#f6f9fb) instead of blue. A blue card on the sky-blue ground loses figure-ground; the classic white index card with blue rules and a red header rule keeps the world and reads as the object.
- Panel typed face: SF Mono (system monospaced) instead of PT Mono. Native menu-bar panels keep the platform's faces; bundling a web font into the app would add weight for no functional gain.
- Hero demo sound is a synthesized approximation labelled as such on the page; bundled pack audio is not served on the site.
